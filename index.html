What love means to me

My love, this is what I believe

Babyy, I want to tell you something about what I believe love really is.

I don't think love is about finding someone who can make us happy every single second, someone who never makes mistakes, or someone who always knows exactly what to say or do. I don't think love is about keeping score of who gives more attention, who puts in more effort, or who loves harder.

To me, love is about two imperfect people choosing to understand each other. It's about accepting that we have different personalities, different ways of expressing affection, different emotions, and sometimes different needs.

Love is being able to communicate when something hurts instead of silently carrying it. It's listening even when we don't completely understand each other. It's apologizing when we're wrong, making an effort to improve, being patient with each other's struggles, and never making the other person feel that they have to earn their place in the relationship.

I believe we should be able to be ourselves with each other. We should be able to have bad days, make mistakes, need personal space, spend time doing our own things, and still know that we are valued.

We shouldn't have to compete with each other or constantly prove that we're good enough. We shouldn't feel like one person has to carry the entire relationship. Both people should be able to express their needs, both should feel heard, and both should be willing to make an effort.

And most importantly, I think love should feel safe, not like an examination where we're constantly wondering whether we're passing or failing.

I don't expect you to be available every moment. I don't expect you to know how to handle every situation perfectly. I don't expect you to express love exactly the way I do.

I want us to learn what makes each other feel loved. I want us to talk about our problems instead of assuming what the other person is thinking. I want us to understand that sometimes we'll need reassurance, sometimes patience, sometimes space, and sometimes simply a little extra affection.

I also understand that I have responsibilities as your boyfriend. I need to listen to you, understand your feelings, respect your boundaries, communicate better, and make sure that your needs matter just as much as mine.

I don't want a relationship where you feel pressured to give me everything. I want a relationship where we both genuinely want to give each other our best, without losing ourselves in the process.

And my princess, please understand this: you don't have to convince yourself that you're not good enough for me. You don't have to decide what I deserve on my behalf.

I want us to make decisions based on what we genuinely feel and want, not because either of us believes we're somehow inadequate.

I still believe love is something we can learn, build, and nurture together. But it takes two people who genuinely want to do that.

That's what love means to me. Not perfection. Not pressure. Not keeping score.

Just two people who respect each other, understand each other, take responsibility for their mistakes, and willingly choose to build something healthy together.
